**YACHT ROCK**

**Episode I: "The Danger Zone"**

---

*FADE IN:*

*A recording studio. 1986. The walls are wood-paneled. A plant is dying in the corner. Soft light filters through venetian blinds like God is disappointed but still watching.*

*MICHAEL McDONALD sits alone at an electric piano. He is not playing. He is just... sitting. His beard flows. His eyes are distant. He looks like a man who has seen smoothness, and has now seen it taken from him.*

*The door opens.*

*KENNY LOGGINS enters.*

*He is wearing a leather jacket.*

*McDONALD sees the jacket.*

*He does not speak.*

*He doesn't have to.*

---

**McDONALD:**
*(quietly)*

Kenny.

**LOGGINS:**
*(defensive, too fast)*

Mike.

**McDONALD:**

That's a new jacket.

**LOGGINS:**

It's just a jacket, Mike.

**McDONALD:**

Is it.

*McDONALD rises slowly. He walks to the window. He looks out at the parking lot. A seagull lands on a Porsche. This is not a metaphor. It's just a seagull.*

**McDONALD:**

I heard the track, Kenny.

**LOGGINS:**
*(swallowing)*

Which track.

**McDONALD:**

You know which track.

*Silence.*

*Somewhere, a synthesizer hums.*

**LOGGINS:**

It's for a movie, Mike. Tom Cruise. Fighter jets. It's just—it's a job. It's a *job.*

**McDONALD:**
*(turning, slowly)*

We used to have jobs too, Kenny.

Smooth jobs.

*He gestures at the piano.*

**McDONALD:**

You remember this? You remember what we built? "What a Fool Believes," Kenny. "This Is It." We had *something.*

**LOGGINS:**

We still have—

**McDONALD:**

DO WE?

*He slams his hand on the piano. A chord rings out. It is, somehow, still smooth. McDONALD looks at his own hand, surprised by its smoothness even in anger.*

**McDONALD:**
*(quieter now)*

I heard the song, Kenny. I heard... "Danger Zone."

*LOGGINS flinches.*

**McDONALD:**

There are guitars in that song, Kenny.

**LOGGINS:**

Mike—

**McDONALD:**

*Distorted* guitars.

**LOGGINS:**

The producer wanted—

**McDONALD:**

THE TEMPO IS 152 BEATS PER MINUTE, KENNY.

*He is shaking.*

**McDONALD:**

Yacht rock is 96 to 110. You *know* this. We *all* know this. You go above 110, you're in the danger zone. You—

*He stops.*

*He realizes what he's said.*

**McDONALD:**

Oh my God. That's why you called it that.

**LOGGINS:**
*(barely a whisper)*

Mike, please.

**McDONALD:**

You knew. You knew what you were doing. You named it after your own betrayal.

---

*LOGGINS collapses into a chair. His leather jacket squeaks against the upholstery. It is the loudest sound in the room. It is not smooth. Leather is never smooth. Leather is rock. Leather is nyacht.*

---

**LOGGINS:**

I didn't have a choice, Mike. The soundtrack money... you know how it is. Yacht rock doesn't sell like it used to. People want *energy* now. They want *synths.* They want men in jets going fast.

**McDONALD:**

And what do *you* want, Kenny?

*LOGGINS looks up. His eyes are wet.*

**LOGGINS:**

I want...

*He trails off.*

**McDONALD:**

Say it.

**LOGGINS:**

I want to be smooth again, Mike.

*McDONALD closes his eyes.*

**McDONALD:**

Then take off the jacket.

*LOGGINS hesitates.*

**McDONALD:**

Take it off, Kenny.

*Slowly, painfully, LOGGINS removes the leather jacket. Underneath, he is wearing a linen shirt. Cream-colored. Relaxed fit. Two buttons undone.*

*McDONALD exhales.*

**McDONALD:**

There he is.

---

*McDONALD sits down at the piano. He plays a chord. E-flat major 7. The smoothest chord. Music theory doesn't rank chords by smoothness, but if it did, this would be the one.*

---

**McDONALD:**

Sing with me.

**LOGGINS:**

Mike, I don't know if I can—

**McDONALD:**

*Sing with me, Kenny.*

*He plays the opening to "What a Fool Believes."*

*LOGGINS opens his mouth.*

*Nothing comes out.*

*He tries again.*

*A croak. A whisper. The falsetto is gone. The smoothness has left his body.*

**LOGGINS:**
*(horrified)*

Mike. Mike, I can't—it's gone. The smooth. It's *gone.*

*McDONALD stops playing.*

*He looks at LOGGINS for a long moment.*

**McDONALD:**

Then God help you, Kenny.

Because the Danger Zone...

*He puts on his sunglasses. Indoors. It doesn't matter.*

**McDONALD:**

...doesn't forgive.

---

*SMASH CUT TO:*

*A MILITARY JET flying through clouds.*

*"Danger Zone" plays.*

*Inside the cockpit, TOM CRUISE mouths the words, unaware of the smoothness that died so he could feel the need for speed.*

---

*CUT BACK TO:*

*The studio. McDONALD is alone again. LOGGINS is gone. Only the leather jacket remains, crumpled on the floor like a shed skin.*

*McDONALD looks at it.*

*He picks it up.*

*He walks to the trash can.*

*He hesitates.*

---

**McDONALD:**
*(to himself)*

Goodbye, Kenny.

*He drops the jacket in the trash.*

*He sits back down at the piano.*

*He plays.*

*Smooth.*

*So smooth.*

*A single tear rolls into his beard, where it is absorbed, because the beard is also smooth.*

---

*FADE TO BLACK.*

*TEXT ON SCREEN:*

*"Kenny Loggins would not return to yacht rock for eleven years."*

*"Michael McDonald is still waiting."*

---

*CREDITS ROLL.*

*"What a Fool Believes" plays.*

*It has always been playing.*

*It will always be playing.*

---

**END OF EPISODE.**
