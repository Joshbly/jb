*FADE IN:*

*The Port of Miami. 6:00 AM. The sun isn't up yet. The sun knows better.*

*A cruise ship looms against the pre-dawn sky. It is white. It is massive. It is named "THE CELESTIAL HARMONY," which is the kind of name chosen by a committee that has never experienced either celestial or harmony.*

*A banner hangs from the gangway:*

**"WELCOME ABOARD SMOOTH VESSEL VII"**

**"A TRANSFORMATIVE YACHT ROCK IMMERSION"**

**"FEBRUARY 14-17"**

*Below that, in smaller text:*

**"ALL RITUALS MANDATORY"**

---

*A black SUV pulls up to the dock.*

*MICHAEL McDONALD steps out. He is wearing sunglasses even though there is no sun. The sunglasses are prophylactic. They prevent futures.*

*KENNY LOGGINS follows. He is holding a coffee. The coffee is already cold. He doesn't know this yet. He will know it soon and it will set the tone for the next seventy-two hours.*

*CHRISTOPHER CROSS emerges last. He stands very still and looks at the ship the way a mouse looks at a snake that hasn't noticed it yet.*

---

**McDONALD:**

They changed the name.

**LOGGINS:**

What was it before?

**McDONALD:**

"Smooth Sailing V."

**LOGGINS:**

This is seven.

**McDONALD:**

They skipped six.

**LOGGINS:**

Why?

**McDONALD:**

No one talks about six.

---

*Cross hasn't moved.*

---

**CROSS:**

What does "all rituals mandatory" mean?

**McDONALD:**

It means nothing. It's marketing.

**CROSS:**

It doesn't feel like marketing.

**McDONALD:**

It's a cruise, Christopher. They use words like "transformative" and "immersive" because "three days trapped on a boat with people who own your greatest hits on multiple formats" doesn't fit on a banner.

---

*A woman approaches.*

*She is forty-seven. Her hair is pulled back with severe precision. She is wearing all white. Not cruise-white. Ritual-white. The white of someone who has made a choice about white and will not be questioned on it.*

*Her name tag reads:*

**DENISE**

**VOYAGE FACILITATOR**

**SMOOTH VESSEL VII**

**INNER CIRCLE**

---

**DENISE:**

Gentlemen. Welcome. We've been expecting you.

**McDONALD:**

Of course you have. We're contracted to be here.

**DENISE:**

Yes. Contracted.

*She says "contracted" the way someone might say "chosen."*

**DENISE:**

I'm Denise. I'll be your liaison for the Voyage. Please follow me. The Opening Alignment begins in forty minutes.

**LOGGINS:**

The what?

**DENISE:**

The Opening Alignment. It's how we begin. It's how we've always begun.

**McDONALD:**

Since when?

**DENISE:**

Since Smooth Vessel III. Before that, we had a Welcome Reception. But the community felt a reception was insufficiently... intentional.

**CROSS:**

Intentional about what?

---

*Denise smiles.*

*The smile is warm.*

*The smile is terrifying because of how warm it is.*

---

**DENISE:**

About smoothness, Mr. Cross. About what we're all here to cultivate.

---

*She turns and walks toward the ship.*

*They follow.*

*They do not know what else to do.*

---

*CUT TO:*

*INT. THE SHIP — ATRIUM*

*The interior of the Celestial Harmony is not what they expected.*

*There are no casinos. No buffets visible. No families in matching t-shirts.*

*Instead: soft lighting. Ambient music—yacht rock, of course, but slowed down, stretched, almost unrecognizable. White fabric draped from the ceiling in waves. The effect is womb-like. The effect is intentional.*

*And the passengers.*

*The passengers are all wearing white.*

*Every single one.*

*Two thousand people in white linen, white cotton, white silk. Moving slowly through the atrium. Speaking in low voices. Nodding at each other with the recognition of the initiated.*

---

**LOGGINS:**

Why is everyone wearing white?

**DENISE:**

It's the Voyage dress code. White represents openness. Receptivity. The blank canvas upon which smoothness is written.

**McDONALD:**

We weren't told about a dress code.

**DENISE:**

Artists are exempt. You're already smooth. You don't need the external signifier.

**CROSS:**

What if we're not?

**DENISE:**

Not what?

**CROSS:**

Smooth. What if we're not actually smooth?

---

*Denise stops walking.*

*She turns.*

*She looks at Cross with an expression that is difficult to read. Confusion? Pity? Something else?*

---

**DENISE:**

Mr. Cross. You wrote "Sailing."

**CROSS:**

I know.

**DENISE:**

"Sailing" is the foundational text of the smooth philosophy.

**CROSS:**

It's a song.

**DENISE:**

It's more than a song. It's a map. A guide. A way of being in the world.

**CROSS:**

I wrote it in an apartment in San Antonio. I'd never been sailing.

**DENISE:**

We know.

**CROSS:**

You know?

**DENISE:**

Of course. That's what makes it miraculous. You channeled something you'd never experienced. You gave us access to a state of being you yourself had never achieved.

---

*She steps closer.*

---

**DENISE:**

That's why you're here, Mr. Cross. Not to perform. To receive.

**CROSS:**

Receive what?

**DENISE:**

What you gave us. We've been holding it for you. For forty-four years.

---

*She smiles again.*

*Cross does not smile back.*

---

**DENISE:**

The Opening Alignment is in the Grand Smooth Hall. Deck 7. I'll escort you.

---

*CUT TO:*

*THE GRAND SMOOTH HALL*

*It was probably once a theater. Now it is something else.*

*The seats have been removed. In their place: cushions. White cushions arranged in concentric circles around a raised platform at the center. The ceiling has been draped with more white fabric, creating the impression of being inside a cloud, or a tent, or a surrender.*

*Two thousand people in white sit on the cushions. Cross-legged. Silent. Waiting.*

*On the platform: three chairs.*

*The yacht rock boys are led to the platform.*

*They sit.*

*Two thousand faces look up at them.*

---

**McDONALD:**

*(whispering)*

This isn't a cruise.

**LOGGINS:**

*(whispering)*

What is it?

**McDONALD:**

*(whispering)*

I don't know. But we should've read the contract more carefully.

---

*A man approaches the platform.*

*He is sixty-three. Bald. Tan. Exceptionally calm. He moves like someone who has eliminated hurry from his life and is better for it. He is wearing white, of course, but his white is slightly more white than everyone else's. The white of someone in charge.*

*His name tag reads:*

**GERALD**

**VOYAGE ELDER**

**SMOOTH VESSEL I-VII**

**FOUNDING MEMBER**

---

**GERALD:**

Brothers and sisters. Welcome to Smooth Vessel VII.

*The crowd responds in unison:*

**CROWD:**

Smooth waters. Calm winds.

**GERALD:**

Today we begin another Voyage. Another opportunity to move closer to the smooth. Another chance to shed the rough, the urgent, the unresolved.

*He gestures to the yacht rockers.*

**GERALD:**

And this year, we are blessed. The sources are with us. The channels through which smoothness first flowed into the world.

*He turns to them.*

**GERALD:**

Michael. Kenny. Christopher. Thank you for being here.

**McDONALD:**

We're... happy to be here.

**GERALD:**

You're uncertain. I can see it. That's natural. You've been living in the rough world. The fast world. The world of tempo and urgency. But for the next three days, you're home.

**LOGGINS:**

Home?

**GERALD:**

You made the music that made us who we are. This ship, this community, this practice—it exists because of you. Everything we've built, everything we've become, flows from what you created.

*He kneels before them.*

*Two thousand people watch.*

---

**GERALD:**

We've been waiting. All of us. Since the first Voyage. Waiting for you to return.

**CROSS:**

Return? We've never been here before.

**GERALD:**

You've always been here, Christopher. In the music. In the practice. In the smoothness we cultivate every day. This is just the first time you've been here in the body.

---

*He stands.*

---

**GERALD:**

Let us begin the Alignment.

---

*The lights dim.*

*A single note plays. Low. Sustained. A synthesizer pad. It doesn't resolve. It just... hovers.*

*Gerald raises his hands.*

---

**GERALD:**

Close your eyes.

*Two thousand people close their eyes.*

*The yacht rockers do not close their eyes.*

*They look at each other.*

---

**GERALD:**

Breathe in the smooth.

*Two thousand people inhale.*

**GERALD:**

Release the rough.

*Two thousand people exhale.*

---

*This continues.*

*Inhale smooth.*

*Exhale rough.*

*Over and over.*

*The yacht rockers watch.*

---

**LOGGINS:**

*(whispering)*

Michael.

**McDONALD:**

*(whispering)*

I know.

**LOGGINS:**

*(whispering)*

What do we do?

**McDONALD:**

*(whispering)*

We survive until docking. Fifty-six hours.

**LOGGINS:**

*(whispering)*

And if we can't survive?

**McDONALD:**

*(whispering)*

Then we become whatever this is.

---

*The breathing continues.*

*The note continues.*

*The yacht rockers sit on their platform, surrounded by two thousand people in white, and realize slowly, terribly, that they are not the entertainers here.*

*They are the relics.*

*They are the saints.*

*And saints don't get to leave.*

---

*CUT TO:*

*LATER. A CORRIDOR.*

*The yacht rockers walk quickly. Denise follows, unperturbed by their pace.*

---

**McDONALD:**

I want to see the contract.

**DENISE:**

The contract?

**McDONALD:**

The performance contract. The one we signed.

**DENISE:**

Of course. I can have a copy sent to your quarters.

**McDONALD:**

I want to see it now.

**DENISE:**

Is something wrong?

**McDONALD:**

I just spent forty-five minutes on a stage while two thousand people breathed at me and called it an "alignment." I'd like to know what else I agreed to.

**DENISE:**

The Alignment is optional for artists.

**McDONALD:**

It didn't feel optional.

**DENISE:**

Participation is optional. Presence is mandatory.

**LOGGINS:**

What's the difference?

**DENISE:**

You don't have to breathe with them. But you have to be there while they breathe.

**CROSS:**

Why?

**DENISE:**

Because they're breathing toward you.

---

*Cross stops walking.*

---

**CROSS:**

What does that mean?

**DENISE:**

The breath practice is a form of directed intention. They're sending smoothness toward you. Toward the sources. It's a gift.

**CROSS:**

I don't want it.

**DENISE:**

It's not about wanting, Mr. Cross. It's about receiving. You gave us the music. We're giving you back what it became.

**CROSS:**

I didn't ask for it to become anything.

**DENISE:**

Artists never do. That's what makes them artists.

---

*She smiles.*

---

**DENISE:**

Your quarters are on Deck 12. The Smooth Deck. I'll show you.

---

*CUT TO:*

*DECK 12 — CORRIDOR*

*The corridor is silent. The carpet is white. The walls are white. The doors are white with small gold numbers.*

*It feels like a hospital.*

*It feels like the afterlife.*

---

**DENISE:**

Mr. McDonald, you're in 1201. Mr. Loggins, 1202. Mr. Cross, 1203.

*She hands them key cards. The key cards are white.*

**DENISE:**

Dinner is at 7 PM in the Reflection Hall. Semi-formal. White preferred but not required for artists.

**McDONALD:**

What if we want to eat in our rooms?

**DENISE:**

The community would be disappointed.

**McDONALD:**

That's not a no.

**DENISE:**

It's strongly discouraged.

**McDONALD:**

But not forbidden?

**DENISE:**

Nothing is forbidden, Mr. McDonald. We're not that kind of community.

**McDONALD:**

What kind of community are you?

**DENISE:**

The kind that makes requests. And trusts that requests will be honored.

---

*She hands McDonald a folder.*

---

**DENISE:**

Your schedule. The highlighted events are community gatherings. The starred events are artist-community interactions. The circled events are...

**McDONALD:**

Let me guess. Rituals.

**DENISE:**

We prefer "practices."

**McDONALD:**

What's the difference?

**DENISE:**

Rituals are about belief. Practices are about behavior. We don't ask you to believe anything, Mr. McDonald. We just ask you to be present while we practice.

---

*She smiles.*

*She has smiled a lot.*

*The smiles are starting to accumulate.*

---

**DENISE:**

I'll see you at dinner. Smooth waters.

---

*She leaves.*

*The three men stand in the corridor.*

---

**LOGGINS:**

I don't like this.

**McDONALD:**

I know.

**LOGGINS:**

It feels like we're in trouble.

**McDONALD:**

We're not in trouble. We're just... providing a service.

**LOGGINS:**

What service?

**McDONALD:**

I don't know yet.

---

*Cross is looking at the folder.*

---

**CROSS:**

Michael.

**McDONALD:**

What?

**CROSS:**

Look at the schedule.

---

*McDonald takes the folder.*

*He opens it.*

*He reads.*

*His face changes.*

---

**LOGGINS:**

What? What is it?

---

*McDonald hands him the folder.*

*Loggins reads.*

---

**LOGGINS:**

Oh no.

**McDONALD:**

Yeah.

**LOGGINS:**

There are seventeen events per day.

**McDONALD:**

Eighteen, counting the night practices.

**LOGGINS:**

The night practices?

**McDONALD:**

There's a 2 AM event called "Moonsmooth."

**LOGGINS:**

What is Moonsmooth?

**McDONALD:**

I don't know and I don't want to know.

---

*Cross takes the folder.*

*He looks at day three.*

---

**CROSS:**

Michael.

**McDONALD:**

What now?

**CROSS:**

The final event. On the last night.

---

*McDonald looks.*

*The final event is circled three times.*

*In gold ink.*

*It reads:*

**"THE RETURNING"**

**"8:00 PM — GRAND SMOOTH HALL"**

**"ALL SOURCES REQUIRED"**

---

**McDONALD:**

The Returning.

**CROSS:**

What are we returning?

**McDONALD:**

I don't think WE'RE returning anything. I think we ARE the thing being returned.

**LOGGINS:**

Returned to what?

---

*McDonald doesn't answer.*

*He doesn't have an answer.*

*He's starting to realize that's the problem.*

---

*CUT TO:*

*INT. McDONALD'S CABIN — NIGHT*

*McDonald sits on the bed.*

*The cabin is nice. Spacious. A porthole shows black water.*

*On the desk: a white envelope.*

*He opens it.*

*Inside: a handwritten note on heavy cream paper.*

---

*He reads:*

**"Michael—**

**We know you have questions. Everyone does, at first. The rough world teaches us to question. To doubt. To resist.**

**But you're not in the rough world now.**

**You're on the water.**

**You're home.**

**Let yourself receive.**

**Smooth waters,**

**Gerald"**

---

*McDonald sets down the note.*

*He looks at the porthole.*

*The black water looks back.*

---

*CUT TO:*

*INT. CROSS'S CABIN — SAME TIME*

*Cross stands at his porthole.*

*He hasn't moved in eleven minutes.*

*On his desk: the same white envelope. The same note.*

*He hasn't opened it.*

*Not the words. But the feeling.*

*The feeling of being wanted for something you don't understand.*

*CUT TO:*

Smooth waters, Mr. Loggins.

*CUT TO:*

*THE REFLECTION HALL — DINNER*

*Round tables. White tablecloths. White flowers. White candles. Two thousand people in white, eating food that is, somehow, also mostly white.*

*The yacht rockers sit at a raised table at the front of the room. They are visible from every angle. They are always visible. Visibility is the point.*

*Gerald sits with them.*

---

**GERALD:**

I hope you're finding your quarters comfortable.

**McDONALD:**

They're fine.

**GERALD:**

The Smooth Deck was designed specifically for artists. We wanted you to feel held.

**McDONALD:**

Held?

**GERALD:**

Supported. Contained. Safe from the rough.

**McDONALD:**

What rough? We're on a boat.

**GERALD:**

The rough isn't a place, Michael. It's a state. A frequency. The energy of the fast world. The urgency of the unsmooth.

---

*A waiter approaches.*

*He sets down plates.*

*The food is chicken breast in a cream sauce with white asparagus and mashed potatoes.*

*Everything is beige.*

---

**LOGGINS:**

Is there a menu, or...

**GERALD:**

The menu is curated. We find that too many choices create friction.

**LOGGINS:**

Friction?

**GERALD:**

Decisions require energy. Energy creates roughness. We try to minimize unnecessary decisions.

**LOGGINS:**

What if I wanted something else?

**GERALD:**

Do you want something else?

**LOGGINS:**

I... no. This is fine.

**GERALD:**

See? The curation works.

---

*They eat in silence for a moment.*

*Around them, the community eats.*

*The community eats slowly.*

*Very slowly.*

*Each bite deliberate.*

*Each chew intentional.*

---

**CROSS:**

Why do they eat so slowly?

**GERALD:**

It's part of the practice. Smoothness isn't just about music. It's about every action. Eating. Walking. Breathing. Speaking. Every motion can be smooth or rough. We try to make every motion smooth.

**CROSS:**

That sounds exhausting.

**GERALD:**

Only at first. After a while, it becomes natural. After a while, roughness becomes exhausting. You find yourself unable to tolerate it.

**McDONALD:**

And then?

**GERALD:**

And then you're one of us.

---

*He says it simply.*

*He says it like it's obvious.*

*He says it like it's already happened.*

---

**GERALD:**

Tomorrow is a full day. Morning Alignment at 7 AM. The Listening Practice at 9. The Discussion Circle at 11. Lunch. The Smoothness Assessment at 2 PM—

**McDONALD:**

The what?

**GERALD:**

The Smoothness Assessment. It's when new members are evaluated. You'll observe. It helps them to have the sources present during evaluation.

**McDONALD:**

We're not evaluating anyone.

**GERALD:**

Of course not. You're witnessing. The evaluation is done by the Elders.

**McDONALD:**

How many Elders are there?

**GERALD:**

Twelve. We've been with the practice since the beginning. Since Smooth Vessel I.

**McDONALD:**

And what happens to people who fail the assessment?

**GERALD:**

No one fails, Michael. Some people just aren't ready yet. They're invited to continue practicing until the next Voyage.

**McDONALD:**

And if they're never ready?

**GERALD:**

Everyone is ready eventually. Smoothness is our natural state. We've just been taught to be rough. The practice un-teaches us.

---

*Cross sets down his fork.*

---

**CROSS:**

I want to go back to my room.

**GERALD:**

Of course. The evening is unstructured. For rest. For reflection. For receiving.

**CROSS:**

For what?

**GERALD:**

Whatever comes to you. Whatever the smooth provides.
