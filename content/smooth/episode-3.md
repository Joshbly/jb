number: 3
title: The Marina Del Rey Harbor Association Annual Meeting
preview: A conference room at the Marina del Rey Yacht Club. A hand-painted banner reads "SMOOTH SEAS, SMOOTH NEIGHBORS." The annual meeting begins.
---

*FADE IN:*

*A conference room at the Marina del Rey Yacht Club. Folding chairs. A long table at the front. A banner hangs behind it, hand-painted, that reads:*

**"SMOOTH SEAS, SMOOTH NEIGHBORS"**

*Beneath that, in smaller letters:*

**"Slip Disputes • Noise Complaints • The Incident"**

---

*The room is full.*

*MICHAEL McDONALD sits in the front row. He is wearing a linen blazer the color of a cloud that has never known suffering. His beard is conditioned. You can tell because it moves like water.*

*CHRISTOPHER CROSS sits three seats away. He has not made eye contact with anyone. He is staring at a clipboard. The clipboard is empty. He is staring at it anyway.*

*KENNY LOGGINS is in the back row.*

*No one has acknowledged that Kenny Loggins is in the back row.*

*This is intentional.*

---

*At the front table sits DARYL HALL. He is the newly elected President of the Harbor Association. Beside him is JOHN OATES. Oates has a gavel. He has been holding the gavel for eleven minutes. He has not used it. He is waiting.*

---

**HALL:**

I'd like to call this meeting to order.

*Oates raises the gavel.*

*He does not bring it down.*

*He holds it there.*

*Suspended.*

*Three seconds pass.*

*Five.*

*Someone coughs.*

*Oates brings it down.*

*It makes almost no sound. The felt pad on the bottom is too thick. This was a choice.*

**OATES:**
*(quietly)*

Order.

---

**HALL:**

Thank you, John.

First item: the waiting list for Slip 14-C.

*A murmur moves through the room like wind through sailcloth.*

**HALL:**

As you know, Slip 14-C has been vacant since the... departure... of Mr. Fagen and Mr. Becker.

**VOICE FROM THE CROWD:**

Where did they go?

**HALL:**

We don't ask that.

**VOICE:**

But—

**HALL:**

*We don't ask that.*

---

*Silence.*

*Somewhere, a halyard clinks against a mast. It is the only honest sound in the room.*

---

**HALL:**

There are four applicants for the slip. I'll read the names.

*He puts on reading glasses. The glasses are unnecessary. His vision is perfect. The glasses are for authority.*

**HALL:**

Michael McDonald.

*McDonald nods. The nod is humble. The humility is also a performance. Everything here is a performance. The marina is a theater and the ocean is the audience and the ocean does not clap.*

**HALL:**

Christopher Cross.

*Cross does not look up from his empty clipboard.*

**HALL:**

Toto.

*A hand goes up in the middle of the room.*

**STEVE LUKATHER:**

Which Toto?

**HALL:**

All of Toto.

**LUKATHER:**

We're applying as a collective?

**HALL:**

That's what the form says.

**LUKATHER:**

Who filled out the form?

*Silence.*

**DAVID PAICH:**
*(from two rows back)*

I thought you filled out the form.

**LUKATHER:**

I thought *you* filled out the form.

**PAICH:**

Steve, I don't even own a pen.

**LUKATHER:**

What do you mean you don't own a pen?

**PAICH:**

I haven't held a writing instrument since 1984. My hands are for keys only now. You know this.

---

*HALL clears his throat.*

---

**HALL:**

The fourth applicant is—

*He pauses.*

*He removes his glasses.*

*He puts them back on.*

**HALL:**

Kenny Loggins.

---

*The room goes cold.*

*Not metaphorically. The air conditioning has kicked on. But also metaphorically.*

*Every head turns to the back row.*

*LOGGINS sits there. He is wearing a leather jacket. Under the leather jacket, he is wearing another leather jacket. Under that jacket, no one knows. No one has ever known.*

---

**McDONALD:**
*(standing, slowly)*

Daryl.

**HALL:**

Michael.

**McDONALD:**

Is this a joke?

**HALL:**

The Harbor Association doesn't joke.

**McDONALD:**

He's not even yacht rock anymore.

**LOGGINS:**
*(from the back, calm)*

I have a boat.

**McDONALD:**

Having a boat doesn't make you yacht rock, Kenny. Having a boat makes you a *boat owner*. Those are different things. A coroner owns a boat. Several coroners own boats.

**LOGGINS:**

My boat is smooth.

**McDONALD:**

Your *boat* is smooth?

**LOGGINS:**

I had it sanded.

**McDONALD:**

That's not what smooth *means*, Kenny.

---

*CHRISTOPHER CROSS stands. He has put down the clipboard. This means something.*

---

**CROSS:**

If I may.

*The room turns.*

*Cross speaks so rarely that when he does, it lands like a pelican on still water. Heavy. Surprising. Somehow still graceful.*

**CROSS:**

I believe the slip should go to whoever among us has demonstrated the most consistent commitment to smoothness over time. Not a single album. Not a single era. A *lifetime* of smooth.

**LOGGINS:**

That's convenient for you, Christopher.

**CROSS:**

Is it?

**LOGGINS:**

You've been smooth since birth. You came out of the womb smooth. The doctors commented on it.

**CROSS:**

That's a rumor.

**LOGGINS:**

Your mother told me.

**CROSS:**

When did you speak to my mother?

**LOGGINS:**

I've spoken to all of your mothers.

---

*A ripple of unease.*

---

**PAICH:**
*(leaning over to Lukather)*

Has he spoken to your mother?

**LUKATHER:**

I don't know. My mother has Alzheimer's.

**PAICH:**

Does she?

**LUKATHER:**

I think so. She keeps forgetting the bridge to "Rosanna."

**PAICH:**

Steve, everyone forgets the bridge to "Rosanna."

**LUKATHER:**

*Porcaro never forgot the bridge.*

**PAICH:**

Porcaro *was* the bridge.

---

*They both look down. A moment of silence for Porcaro. The moment is smooth. Porcaro would have wanted that.*

---

**HALL:**

We're getting off track.

**OATES:**

*(quietly)*

We were never on track. There is no track. The track is a lie we tell ourselves so we can pretend meetings end.

**HALL:**

John.

**OATES:**

I've been to six hundred meetings, Daryl. Not one of them has ended. They just pause. They pause indefinitely. And then we call the pause an "adjournment" and we go home and we lie in our beds and we think about the meetings and the meetings think about us.

---

*Hall stares at Oates.*

*Oates stares at the gavel.*

*The gavel stares at nothing. It is a gavel.*

---

**McDONALD:**

I'd like to make a motion.

**HALL:**

Go ahead.

**McDONALD:**

I move that Kenny Loggins be disqualified from consideration for Slip 14-C on the grounds of historical nyacht behavior.

*Gasps.*

**LOGGINS:**

You can't do that.

**McDONALD:**

Article Seven, Section Four of the Harbor Association Bylaws. "Any member who has engaged in documented nyacht activity may be subject to slip denial pending review."

**LOGGINS:**

That bylaw was written in 1986.

**McDONALD:**

The bylaws don't expire, Kenny.

**LOGGINS:**

I wasn't even a member in 1986.

**McDONALD:**

The bylaws don't require membership, Kenny. They require *smoothness.* And in 1986, you were in a *zone*, Kenny. A *danger* zone.

---

*LOGGINS stands.*

*The leather jackets creak.*

*It is the least smooth sound any of them have ever heard.*

---

**LOGGINS:**

I did what I had to do.

**McDONALD:**

Did you?

**LOGGINS:**

Tom Cruise asked me personally.

**McDONALD:**

Tom Cruise has asked a lot of people a lot of things, Kenny. That doesn't make it smooth.

**LOGGINS:**

The song went to number one.

**McDONALD:**

Number one isn't smooth. Number one is *loud.* Number one is a chart position. Smooth is a *feeling.* Smooth is what happens in the spaces between chart positions. Smooth is the water under the boat, not the boat itself.

---

*CROSS nods.*

*PAICH nods.*

*Even OATES nods, slightly, with the gavel still in his hand, as if the gavel itself is agreeing.*

---

**LOGGINS:**

You wrote "Yah Mo B There."

**McDONALD:**

What about it?

**LOGGINS:**

That song is nine minutes long, Michael.

**McDONALD:**

And?

**LOGGINS:**

Nine minutes is not smooth. Nine minutes is *prog.*

---

*A gasp.*

*Someone drops a glass of white wine.*

*The glass does not shatter. It lands on carpet. Everything in this room is designed to absorb impact. That is also a metaphor.*

---

**McDONALD:**

You take that back.

**LOGGINS:**

I won't.

**McDONALD:**

"Yah Mo B There" won a Grammy.

**LOGGINS:**

"Danger Zone" was in a movie, Michael. *Movies are forever.*

**McDONALD:**

Grammys are also forever.

**LOGGINS:**

Grammys are a statue. Movies are a *feeling.*

**McDONALD:**

You just said smooth is a feeling.

**LOGGINS:**

There can be multiple feelings, Michael.

**McDONALD:**

No. There can't. There's smooth. And there's everything else. And you chose everything else.

---

*LOGGINS reaches into his jacket.*

*Everyone tenses.*

*He pulls out a photograph.*

---

**LOGGINS:**

This is a picture of my boat.

**McDONALD:**

I don't want to see your boat.

**LOGGINS:**

Her name is *Smooth Operator.*

**McDONALD:**

That's a Sade song.

**LOGGINS:**

I know.

**McDONALD:**

You named your boat after a Sade song?

**LOGGINS:**

I tried to license "What a Fool Believes." You said no.

**McDONALD:**

I said no because you're not smooth, Kenny.

**LOGGINS:**

I was *trying* to become smooth.

**McDONALD:**

You can't *try* to become smooth. Smooth isn't a destination. Smooth is a way of moving through the world. You either are or you aren't. You either wake up and the light through your window is soft and forgiving, or you wake up and the light is harsh and you reach for a leather jacket.

---

*Loggins looks at the photograph.*

*He looks at McDonald.*

*He looks at the room full of people who used to be his collaborators, his friends, his harmonizing partners.*

---

**LOGGINS:**

I just wanted a place to park.

---

*Silence.*

*Somehow, this is the saddest thing anyone has ever said.*

---

**CROSS:**

*(standing again)*

Kenny.

**LOGGINS:**

Don't.

**CROSS:**

I'll share my slip.

*The room erupts.*

**McDONALD:**

Christopher, you can't—

**CROSS:**

Slip 22-B is wide enough for two vessels if we moor stern-to-bow. I've done the calculations.

**PAICH:**

On what?

**CROSS:**

*(holding up his clipboard)*

**PAICH:**

That clipboard is empty.

**CROSS:**

The calculations are in my head. The clipboard is for *authority.*

---

*HALL stands.*

---

**HALL:**

Christopher, this is highly irregular.

**CROSS:**

I am highly irregular, Daryl. I won five Grammys in one night, including Best New Artist, and then I became a ghost. I have been a ghost for forty years. I haunt yacht rock like a benevolent spirit. I ask for nothing. I expect nothing. I simply *am.* And if I want to share my slip with a man who lost his way, that is my right as a ghost.

---

*No one knows how to respond to this.*

---

**OATES:**

*(quietly, to Hall)*

He's right. The bylaws don't prohibit slip-sharing among consenting members.

**HALL:**

John, whose side are you on?

**OATES:**

I'm on the side of this meeting ending, Daryl. I have been on that side since 1977.

---

*LOGGINS walks forward.*

*The leather creaks with every step.*

*He reaches CROSS.*

*They look at each other.*

---

**CROSS:**

You'll have to take off the jacket.

**LOGGINS:**

Which one?

**CROSS:**

Both of them.

**LOGGINS:**

Christopher, I haven't—

**CROSS:**

I know. But the marina has rules. And the rules say: no leather south of the parking lot.

---

*LOGGINS hesitates.*

*He looks at McDonald.*

*McDonald looks away.*

*LOGGINS looks at the room.*

*The room looks back.*

---

*Slowly, LOGGINS removes the outer leather jacket.*

*He folds it.*

*He sets it on a chair.*

*He removes the inner leather jacket.*

*Beneath it is a shirt.*

*The shirt is linen.*

*It is cream-colored.*

*Two buttons are undone.*

---

*McDONALD sees the shirt.*

*His eyes widen.*

---

**McDONALD:**

Kenny.

**LOGGINS:**

I know.

**McDONALD:**

You kept it.

**LOGGINS:**

I never stopped keeping it.

---

*It is the shirt Loggins wore on the cover of their 1978 duet album.*

*It has been waiting.*

*It has always been waiting.*

---

*CROSS extends his hand.*

*LOGGINS takes it.*

---

**CROSS:**

Welcome back.

---

*OATES raises the gavel.*

*He brings it down.*

*Still almost no sound.*

*But this time, somehow, it's enough.*

---

**OATES:**

Meeting adjourned.

---

*FADE TO BLACK.*

*TEXT ON SCREEN:*

*"Kenny Loggins was granted provisional mooring rights in Slip 22-B."*

*"The arrangement lasted four months."*

*"Then the 'Top Gun: Maverick' soundtrack happened."*

*"Christopher Cross has not spoken publicly since."*

---

*One more line:*

*"The shirt is still in the marina lost-and-found."*

*"No one has claimed it."*

*"Everyone knows whose it is."*

---

**END OF EPISODE.**

---

*POST-CREDITS SCENE:*

*An empty dock. Night. The water is black and still.*

*STEELY DAN—FAGEN and BECKER—emerge from the shadows. They are wearing matching black turtlenecks. They are always wearing matching black turtlenecks. No one knows where they buy them. The store doesn't exist on any map.*

---

**FAGEN:**

They voted on the slip.

**BECKER:**

We heard.

**FAGEN:**

They gave it to Toto.

**BECKER:**

Which Toto?

**FAGEN:**

All of Toto.

**BECKER:**

*(nodding slowly)*

They'll never agree on who drives the boat.

**FAGEN:**

No.

**BECKER:**

Porcaro would've known.

**FAGEN:**

Porcaro always knew.

---

*They stand there.*

*The water laps at the dock.*

*A single light blinks in the distance.*

*It could be a buoy.*

*It could be something else.*

---

**BECKER:**

Should we go back?

**FAGEN:**

To the marina?

**BECKER:**

To *anything.*

---

*Long pause.*

---

**FAGEN:**

No.

**BECKER:**

No.

---

*They turn.*

*They walk into the darkness.*

*They do not look back.*

*They have never looked back.*

*Looking back is not smooth.*

*Looking back is nyacht.*

---

**ACTUAL END OF EPISODE.**
