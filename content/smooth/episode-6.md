**YACHT ROCK**

**Episode VI: "The Brunch"**

---

*FADE IN:*

*A restaurant. West Hollywood. 11:47 AM. Sunday.*

*The restaurant is called "Omelette Du Fromage." The name is grammatically incorrect. No one has ever mentioned this. The omelets are seventeen dollars. The mimosas are bottomless. The clientele is exclusively people who peaked in a specific window of time and are now navigating the aftermath.*

*Seated at a corner booth:*

*MICHAEL McDONALD.*

*KENNY LOGGINS.*

*CHRISTOPHER CROSS.*

*They are not speaking.*

*They have been not speaking for eleven minutes.*

*This is normal.*

*Brunch requires silence first. The silence is where the eggs happen.*

---

*A WAITER approaches. He is twenty-three. He has a tiny mustache that looks like it's still deciding whether to commit. His name tag says "TYLER."*

---

**McDONALD:**

Bottomless?

Of course.

*Tyler leaves.*

---

**McDONALD:**

What reunion?

Hm.

Time moves differently for us.

Christopher, please.

**CROSS:**

I'm just saying. After "Sailing," I lost about a decade. I blinked and it was 1994. I blinked again and everyone had email.

---

*The door to the restaurant opens.*

*A figure enters.*

*He is wearing sunglasses indoors. His hair is perfect. TOO perfect. Like a simulation of hair. Like what an alien would design if you described hair to them over a bad phone connection.*

*It is RICHARD MARX.*

---

**MARX:**

Michael.

**McDONALD:**

Richard.

**MARX:**

I heard there was a brunch.

**McDONALD:**

This is a private brunch.

**MARX:**

I don't believe in private brunches.

---

*He sits down. In the booth. Next to Cross. Cross does not acknowledge him. Cross acknowledges very little.*

---

**MARX:**

What are we discussing?

**McDONALD:**

We're not discussing anything with you, Richard.

**MARX:**

Is it the reunion? I want in on the reunion.

**LOGGINS:**

There's no reunion.

**MARX:**

Michael just said there was a reunion.

**LOGGINS:**

Did he?

**McDONALD:**

I mentioned it in passing.

**MARX:**

I should be on the reunion tour.

**McDONALD:**

Richard, we've discussed this.

**MARX:**

I wrote "Right Here Waiting."

**McDONALD:**

We know.

**MARX:**

That song was number one for three weeks.

**McDONALD:**

We know.

**MARX:**

People proposed to that song, Michael. People conceived children to that song. Entire *marriages* are built on that song.

**McDONALD:**

And yet.

**MARX:**

And yet WHAT.

---

*McDONALD sighs.*

*This is a conversation they have had before.*

*This is a conversation they will have again.*

*Some conversations are eternal. This is one of them.*

---

**McDONALD:**

Richard, "Right Here Waiting" is a power ballad.

**MARX:**

So?

**McDONALD:**

Yacht rock is not power ballads.

**MARX:**

What's the difference?

**McDONALD:**

The difference is *power,* Richard. Power ballads have power. They build. They crescendo. They have key changes in the final chorus.

**MARX:**

"Right Here Waiting" doesn't have a key change.

**McDONALD:**

It has key change *energy,* Richard. It has the *threat* of a key change. You can feel it lurking. The whole song is just waiting to go up a half step. It's *tense.*

**MARX:**

Tense?

**McDONALD:**

YACHT ROCK ISN'T TENSE, RICHARD. Yacht rock is RESOLVED. Yacht rock is what happens AFTER the tension. Yacht rock is the cigarette after the—

**LOGGINS:**

Michael.

**McDONALD:**

I'm making a point.

**LOGGINS:**

You're making a point in a restaurant.

---

*TYLER appears.*

---

**TYLER:**

Another mimosa for the table?

**McDONALD:**

Please.

**TYLER:**

And for you, sir?

*He looks at Marx.*

**MARX:**

Do you know who I am?

**TYLER:**

No.

**MARX:**

I'm Richard Marx.

**TYLER:**

...okay.

**MARX:**

"Right Here Waiting"?

**TYLER:**

Is that... is that a thing you're doing? Like, right here waiting for your drink?

**MARX:**

It's a SONG, Tyler.

**TYLER:**

How do you know my name?

**MARX:**

Your NAME TAG, Tyler.

**TYLER:**

Oh, right. What can I get you?

**MARX:**

I'll have whatever he's having.

*He points at Cross.*

**TYLER:**

Avocado toast with existential anonymity?

**CROSS:**

He can't have that. He hasn't earned it.

**MARX:**

I've sold fifty million records.

**CROSS:**

And yet no one in this restaurant knows who you are.

**MARX:**

YOU know who I am.

**CROSS:**

That's my curse, Richard. I know everyone. I remember everyone. I am the institutional memory of soft rock. I carry you all with me, everywhere, like a suitcase full of harmonies I can never put down.

---

*Silence.*

---

**TYLER:**

I'll just get you an omelette.

*He leaves.*

---

**MARX:**

I just want to be included.

**McDONALD:**

Richard—

**MARX:**

Do you know what it's like? To be almost smooth? To be *adjacent* to smooth? I had the hair. I had the production values. I had the piano-driven arrangements. I had ALL THE INGREDIENTS. And somehow, SOMEHOW, it adds up to "not yacht rock."

**LOGGINS:**

I've been there, Richard.

**MARX:**

You HAVE yacht rock songs, Kenny. "This Is It." "I'm Alright."

**LOGGINS:**

"I'm Alright" is from Caddyshack.

**MARX:**

So?

**LOGGINS:**

It's a comedy song, Richard. About gophers.

**MARX:**

It's still smooth.

**LOGGINS:**

It's smooth in the verses. The chorus is basically just me yelling.

**MARX:**

At least you GET a chorus.

*The door opens again.*

*Another figure enters.*

*This one is wearing a leather jacket. Under the leather jacket is a denim jacket. Under the denim jacket, theoretically, is a body, but no one has ever confirmed this.*

*Not THE Bon Jovi.*

*JON Bon Jovi.*

*The man himself.*

---

**BON JOVI:**

I heard there was a brunch.

**McDONALD:**

HOW DO PEOPLE KEEP HEARING ABOUT THIS BRUNCH?

**BON JOVI:**

**EVERYONE:**

No.

**McDONALD:**

STRINGS DON'T MAKE SOMETHING SMOOTH, JON.

What does make something smooth?

**McDONALD:**

NOTHING MAKES SOMETHING SMOOTH. SMOOTH EITHER IS OR IT ISN'T. SMOOTH IS NOT ACHIEVED. SMOOTH IS REVEALED.

---

*He's standing now.*

*He's breathing heavily.*

*TYLER approaches with a tray of food.*

---

**TYLER:**

Everything okay here?

**McDONALD:**

No, Tyler. Everything is not okay. I came here for a quiet brunch with my colleagues. A brunch about EGGS. About MIMOSAS. About gently discussing a REUNION TOUR that may or may not happen depending on venue availability and everyone's THERAPY SCHEDULES. And instead I'm being AMBUSHED by ADJACENT ARTISTS who want VALIDATION that I CANNOT PROVIDE.

**TYLER:**

...should I come back?

**McDONALD:**

No. Leave the food. Leave all of it.

---

*Tyler sets down the plates.*

*He backs away.*

*He does not ask if they need anything else.*

*He knows they need things that he cannot give.*

---

**BON JOVI:**

I just want to know why.

**McDONALD:**

Why what?

**BON JOVI:**

Why some of us and not others. We all came up at the same time. We all had the same haircuts. We all played the same radio formats. Why do YOU get to be smooth and I don't?

**McDONALD:**

Because you played stadiums, Jon.

**BON JOVI:**

So did you.

**McDONALD:**

No. I played AMPHITHEATERS. There's a difference.

**BON JOVI:**

What's the difference?

**McDONALD:**

Amphitheaters have GRASS SEATING, Jon. People bring PICNIC BLANKETS. They drink WINE. Stadiums have PYROTECHNICS.

**BON JOVI:**

I've cut back on the pyrotechnics.

**McDONALD:**

You can't cut back on pyrotechnics. Once you've had pyrotechnics, the pyrotechnics are IN you. The fire is IN you. You could play an acoustic set in a LIBRARY and people would still expect something to EXPLODE.

---

*CROSS speaks. Quietly. Everyone stops.*

---

**CROSS:**

There's a test.

**BON JOVI:**

What?

**CROSS:**

There's a way to know. If you're smooth.

**MARX:**

What is it?

**CROSS:**

You imagine yourself on a boat. A small boat. A sailboat. It's sunset. You're alone. There's a gentle wind. Not enough to really sail anywhere, but enough to feel. The water is calm. There's a drink in your hand. Something clear. Something cold.

And you ask yourself: in this moment, on this boat, at this sunset... what song is playing?

If the answer is YOUR song... you're smooth.

If the answer is someone else's song... you're not.

---

*Silence.*

---

**BON JOVI:**

...I see "Livin' on a Prayer."

**CROSS:**

On a sailboat?

**BON JOVI:**

**CROSS:**

Then no.

**MARX:**

**MARX:**

**CROSS:**

Then no.

*He takes a sip of his margarita.*

*The margarita has arrived impossibly fast.*

*No one saw Tyler bring it.*

*Maybe Tyler didn't bring it.*

*Maybe it just materialized.*

---

**BUFFETT(?)**:

You boys spend so much time arguing about what's smooth. Meanwhile, I spent forty years actually BEING smooth. Not in the songs. In the LIFE. I built an empire out of flip-flops and tequila. I made "casual" into a philosophy. I died doing exactly what I wanted to do, which was nothing in particular, in a very pleasant location.

**McDONALD:**

**BUFFETT(?)**:

Am I? Does it matter? I'm here. I'm having a margarita. It's almost noon on a Sunday. What more is there?

*He leaves a hundred-dollar bill on the table.*

*The bill is slightly damp, like it was recently retrieved from a pocket near the ocean.*

---

**BUFFETT(?)**:

Enjoy your brunch, gentlemen. Enjoy your arguments. Enjoy your categories and your certifications and your endless debates about who's in and who's out.

But remember:

*He turns at the door.*

And the water doesn't care what tempo you're playing.
